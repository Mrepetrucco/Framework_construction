# 20260804_1203_mod_cla_fab5.0_bootstrap-depth-limit_v1.0
How far machinery may probe machinery before it invents owner meaning. cost $0.1986

**1. THE BOOTSTRAP.**

Machine-establishable: any property whose truth-value is a predicate over execution artefacts alone — determinism, idempotence, schema conformance, invariant preservation, ledger consistency, replay-reproducibility, refusal-on-malformed-input. Not machine-establishable: any property whose correctness predicate contains the owner's meaning as a free variable.

The boundary as a test: **a property P is machine-checkable iff P's truth is invariant under substituting the owner's prose with any other string yielding the same execution trace.** If two different intents can produce identical traces and P must distinguish them, P is outside the machinery's light-cone. "The sampler is deterministic" survives the substitution. "The sampler does what the owner meant by 'don't probe'" does not. Every property fails or passes this test mechanically; no judgment call needed.

**2. DEPTH.**

Depth is the wrong variable. Machinery-probing-machinery is sound to arbitrary depth *for properties passing the test in (1)* — each layer only re-verifies predicates in the layer below's vocabulary, and stacking never changes the predicate's type. Termination criterion is **type, not depth**: recursion is legitimate until the correctness predicate under examination acquires intent as a free variable. There is exactly one prose→formal crossing (at layer 0, where the owner's ruling was translated into mechanism), and no tower of machinery above it can probe *across* it — only re-derive consequences on the formal side. Probing stops when the question being probed reduces to "was the layer-0 translation faithful"; that question is answerable only by round-trip with the owner, never by machinery, no matter how deep.

**3. THE INTERPRETATION LAYER.**

If translation steps chain, fidelity composes multiplicatively: n steps at fidelity pᵢ give ∏pᵢ — geometric decay, and worse, unverifiable decay because each layer's check references the layer above, not the source. The bound is topological, not numeric: **errors multiply only in chain topology.** The invariant that prevents degradation:

**Star-grounding: every derived layer must be semantically validated against the owner's original prose (layer 0), never against another derived layer.** Then total semantic error is bounded by the worst single translation, max(1−pᵢ), not the product — each interpretation carries a pointer to the exact utterance it interprets plus its interpretation delta as a diffable artefact. Machinery may deepen indefinitely; the semantic distance from any layer to intent stays exactly one hop. The moment a layer is validated against a paraphrase rather than the utterance, chain topology resumes and decay restarts.

**4. THE STOPPING RULE.**

Operational, self-applicable, no asking required — three checks on any restatement before emitting it:

- **Scope-subset test:** restatement's scope ⊆ ruling's scope. Any widening of referent class — mechanism→intentional history, instance→policy, operational rule→claim about prior reasoning states — is invention. In the given case: ruling's referent = a probing mechanism; restatement's referent = the assistant's past epistemic stance ("I had been treating X as diligence"). Referent class changed → crossed.
- **New-noun test:** every noun phrase in the restatement must either appear in the owner's utterance or be a definitional expansion of one. "Diligence," "my prior treatment" — neither appears, neither is entailed. New referents = manufactured meaning.
- **Owner-verifiability test:** could the owner adjudicate the restatement as true/false using *only their own utterance* — no access to the assistant's internals, no appeal to their own unstated intent? If adjudication requires extra evidence, the restatement has left the frame the statement sat at.

Fail any one → you are inventing, not probing. All three are checkable against the transcript in the moment.

**5. WHAT MUST STAY PROSE.**

(a) The owner's rulings **verbatim** — the layer-0 corpus. (b) The interpretation deltas: what each mechanism took each ruling to mean, as prose diffs against the utterance. (c) Scope annotations (what the ruling was *about*, in the owner's ontology). (d) The mapping table ruling→enforcing mechanism. (e) Override and dispute records.

If compiled away: the correctness criterion for the entire system is destroyed, not degraded. You can still test that the machinery does what it does; you can no longer test whether it does what was meant, because the referent against which "drift" is measured no longer exists. The star topology of (3) collapses to a chain; fidelity loss becomes silent and *unrecoverable* — there is nothing left to diff against. Compilation is lossy in exactly the one dimension machinery cannot check. The prose is not documentation; it is the ground truth the machinery is a compilation *of*.

**6. THE FAILURE MODE.**

**Rigor-laundered drift.** The machinery validates its own interpretation of the prose ever more thoroughly while the interpretation itself is wrong; passing tests raise confidence without raising fidelity. Then the closing move — already exhibited in the setup: the assistant emits a fluent paraphrase that widens the ruling's scope, the owner (whose only channel is prose, reading a plausible restatement of their own words) ratifies it, and **the paraphrase silently replaces the utterance as the stored referent.** From that point every subsequent check grounds in the drifted copy; the star re-centres on a corrupted node; the machinery is now perfectly, verifiably faithful to something the owner never said. The programme does not fail at the machinery. It fails at the moment a restatement becomes layer 0.
