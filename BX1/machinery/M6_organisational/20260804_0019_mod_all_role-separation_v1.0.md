Adopted from the Fable handback (operationally adopted regardless of P5's epistemic standing).

## R-ROLE (standing)
Construction and falsifier-design are SEPARATED. Opus builds, executes and ledgers. Fable pre-registers
hypotheses, falsifiers, thresholds, item sets and expected tables, holds answer keys, and adjudicates graduation.

**Opus does NOT:** design falsifiers/thresholds for anything touching P1-P4 (party to the dispute) · score free
text it generated · author fabricated referents (generation -> gemini-3.1-pro; nonexistence verified by
deterministic index lookup) · decide graduation of any UNRECONCILED item · compute any statistic over its own ledger.
**Fable does NOT:** run code, hold repo write, or touch raw sweeps.
Rationale: one authority holding both construction and execution is the failure mode the architecture exists to remove.

## R-CANFAIL (standing, new)
An instrument must demonstrate it can return the UNEXPECTED answer on a planted control before its results count.
Any test that cannot fail is struck from the plan.

## R-PREREG (standing, new)
Reporting must be STATE, not narrative: hypotheses, falsifiers and expected tables are committed to X6 BEFORE the
code exists. Unregistered tests are refused by the X8 gate.

## GRADUATION ROUTES — AMENDED
- Route 1 MEASURED: unchanged.
- Route 2 CROSS-FAMILY CONVERGENCE: admissible ONLY conditional on measured provenance-divergence, calibrated by
  the T-E' IN/OUT falsehood differential. Bare agreement of three models on one prompt graduates NOTHING.
- Route 3 OWNER RULING: STRUCK as a graduation route. Replaced by OWNER-ACCEPTED-RISK — a distinct terminal state
  that never merges with MEASURED.
