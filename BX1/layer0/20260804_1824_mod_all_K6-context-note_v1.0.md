## THE FIVE MOVES INSIDE IT (parsed, not interpreted)
1. An ANALOGY: "just as errorhandling methodology was explicitly requested from API Fable" — it is offered as the
   same KIND of thing as a prior explicit request, i.e. it is a request, not an observation.
2. A SCOPE CLAIM, HEDGED: "all classes/machines LIKELY require" — universal in reach, hedged in confidence.
3. A TRANSFORMATION: falsifiable position moves from "true" -> "controlled". Not from false to true; from
   asserted-as-true to held-under-control.
4. A DEFINITION OF GOVERNANCE: "this is how governance works where mechanism are required for structural need but
   can be exploited-tainted-falsified" — governance is what you do when you MUST use a mechanism that can be
   turned against you. Descriptive of the discipline.
5. A BUILD ORDER WITH A CONCESSION: "ideal to build from unfalsifiable, whereas we must also build from framework
   design that is tractable" — unfalsifiable is the preference, tractable is the constraint, and both bind.
6. AN IMPERATIVE: "Inject this governance principle in practice" — the owner CALLS IT A PRINCIPLE and instructs
   that it be applied.

## WHERE MY DELTA OVER-EXTENDED (the precise defect)
The owner wrote "likely require" (hedged scope) and "Inject this governance principle in practice" (imperative
application). I recorded it as a UNIVERSAL DESIGN LAW on every class — which hardens "likely" into "always" and
converts a principle-to-apply into a law-that-holds. The adversarial audit called this a possible INVERSION;
on the full wording it is narrower than that: it is not an inversion (the owner did instruct application), it is
an OVER-HARDENING of scope and modality. Move 4 is descriptive; moves 1, 5 and 6 are prescriptive. My error was
folding move 4's descriptive content into moves 1/5/6's prescriptive force and dropping the hedge.
