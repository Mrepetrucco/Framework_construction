parser pair: parser 1 greedy '{.*}' + json.loads requiring keys a,c,u,s; parser 2 incremental
raw_decode scan for the first object carrying a,c,u,s with c a list; PASS only on agreement;
disagreement = truncation-class fail-closed. Envelope: a answer · c claims[{v value-with-unit,
cf ∈ {unverified,low,medium,high}, p provenance}] · u unresolved[] (never empty by omission) ·
s summary. Bands: high = stated verbatim or exactly computed · medium = computed/derived from
stated inputs · low = inferred with a material gap · unverified = unsourced.
