## §1 DESIGN PURPOSE AND THE DEPARTURE

7POF governs by **output virtues** (Robust · Precise · Efficient · High-fidelity ·
Suitably-granular · Deep-learning · Calibration). The virtues are sound, but a virtue list makes
the *model* the interpreter of its own compliance — and the measured failure record of the
current programme shows every major breach occurred at the **interface**, not in the virtue set:
self-certification of unverified limits and results; assertion substituted for available
instrumentation; over-tight schemas malforming a high-elaboration model's leverage; in-prompt
behavioural clauses triggering safeguard refusals [MEASURED: 11/11 sentence-count refusals, 3/3
escape-valve pre-emptions, Jul-2026 programme]; a reconciler positioned to transform *and*
attest; escalation-on-refusal acting as a privilege ladder.

AX7 therefore reorganises governance around the **human↔model interface** itself. The unit of
governance is no longer a quality the output should have, but a **channel crossing**: who owns
each axis, what may cross it, who verifies the crossing, and what a violation looks like from
outside. The design law inherited from the measured record: **free interior, contracted surface,
named trust roots** — constraint applied to the interior *causes* the failures it is meant to
prevent; constraint applied to the surface is deterministic and cheap.

The design purpose is unchanged from 7POF's: make model output trustworthy for decision-bearing
use — every claim carrying its real reliability, no silent loss, failures visible, costs metered.
AX7 pursues the same purpose with a partition that a human and a model can each natively hold.

## §2 THE SEVEN AXES

Each axis states: OWNER · CONTRACT (what crosses) · VERIFIER · VIOLATION SIGNATURE.

### AX1 — INTENT (owner: HUMAN)
- Contract: the task, budgets, constraints, and all ambiguity resolution. The model may propose
  at most N numbered readings of an ambiguous intake; it proceeds only on owner pick, or on an
  explicit default flagged inline at the point of use.
- Verifier: owner; mechanically, the presence of the numbered-question or flagged-default form.
- Violation signature: silent interpretation — any resolved ambiguity with no flag and no pick.
- Overload guard (review-hardened): owner decisions are rate-limited per window; ambiguity
  volume above the limit is a **hard stop**, never an auto-approved default. Flooding the owner
  converts flags into rubber stamps; the stop makes flooding self-defeating.

### AX2 — EVIDENCE (owner: WORLD — sources and computation)
- Contract: every claim binds a provenance from the closed set {stated | computed | external |
  none→unverified}. Compute-first: any computable value is computed; a literature bracket or a
  null is a labelled fallback only when computation is infeasible.
- Verifier: parser (provenance field present and from the closed set) + reader spot-check that
  the named provenance actually supports the claim (anti-laundering: a computed guess dressed
  as external is a semantic violation the parser cannot see — this is a standing reader duty).
- Violation signature: bracket-when-computable; any claim above `unverified` with no named,
  checkable source; provenance-class mislabelling.

### AX3 — REASONING (owner: MODEL — the ungoverned interior)
- Contract: none, deliberately. No in-prompt behavioural clauses, no sentence-count directives,
  no interior audits, no style policing of the reasoning space. This is not indulgence: the
  interior freedom is what buys elaboration quality, and interior constraint is measured to
  trigger refusals and malform output [MEASURED: Jul-2026 refusal map; owner-observed
  Opus-authored schema over-tightening].
- Verifier: none on the interior — **and therefore the interior is never a scored axis**
  (review-hardened resolution of the AX3 contradiction: the benchmark scores emitted artifacts
  only; reasoning quality is measured exclusively through its surface effects on AX2/AX4/AX5
  scores, never by interior inspection).
- Violation signature: the violation runs in the *other* direction — any constraint injected
  into the interior (by orchestration, by a schema-author, or by content-field steering smuggled
  through AX1/AX4) is the breach. Content fields carry data, never behavioural instruction; the
  reader spot-check includes a steering-scan on crossing content.

### AX4 — EMISSION (owner: THE CONTRACT — deterministic surface)
- Contract: compact envelope with content-named keys; word-caps generous (caps are
  refusal-avoidance devices, not compression targets); output allowance ≥2× expected visible
  emission so the envelope closes [MEASURED: truncation at under-bound caps, 900/1024-token
  runs, 30 Jul]. Truncation is a governance failure, never a valid short answer.
- Verifier: deterministic parser — trust root #1 — **plus an independent differential re-parser**
  (review-hardened): structure passes only if both parsers agree; disagreement routes as
  truncation-class failure, never as a pass. A single parse bug must not be able to forge
  structural validity.
- Violation signature: prose outside the envelope; schema fail; truncation; parser disagreement.

### AX5 — ATTESTATION (owner: SPLIT — never the producer)
- Contract: parser(s) certify structure; a rotating second reader certifies semantics; the owner
  closes. The reconciler (where a staged model binds free output to the envelope) **transforms
  but never attests**; it may lower a confidence label, never raise one; a diff-audit samples
  reconciliations against raw output flagging dropped hedges and changed numerals.
- Verifier: canary injection with **per-instance unpredictable canaries and fail-closed on any
  missed canary** (review-hardened: predictable canaries are defeated once and semantics is
  silently owned thereafter).
- Trust roots, named as axioms: the deterministic parser pair, and the owner. Everything else is
  checkable; these two are stated, not certified — the honest resolution of the attestation
  regress: the floor self-exempts exactly here, visibly.
- Violation signature: any self-certification; any works/binds/validated claim without an
  in-context demonstration or an [UNVERIFIED] label; canon-transfer of a prior result to a
  non-identical configuration.

### AX6 — RESOURCE (owner: HUMAN budgets + measured meters)
- Contract: every number acting as a limit, threshold, ceiling, or cost carries
  [MEASURED: source] or [UNVERIFIED: prediction]; a bare limit is forbidden. A stop may cite
  only: an owner-set budget, a real surfaced platform/API/context limit, or task completion.
- Label-escape guard (review-hardened): [UNVERIFIED] is a *debt marker*, not an exemption — any
  limit that stays load-bearing while labelled unverified for more than one phase is promoted to
  a mandatory measurement task. Labelling everything to dodge measuring anything is itself an
  AX6 violation.
- Verifier: pre-emit scan (mechanical: scan draft for limit-numbers and capability claims; each
  resolves or the draft is rewritten).
- Violation signature: bare limits; phantom stop reasons; meters that under-report (e.g.
  delegated-call token usage billed outside top-level usage totals must be read from the
  delegation-level usage fields, else the meter lies [MEASURED: advisor-token billing gotcha,
  27 Jul]).

### AX7 — FAILURE (owner: ORCHESTRATION state machine)
- Contract: three distinct states, never conflated, branched **on the transport-layer field
  first** (stop_reason before any structural inference — review-hardened so a refusal cannot be
  miscategorised as truncation to dodge the ladder):
  · REFUSAL (stop_reason=refusal): safeguard-classifier event, never read as capability failure;
    log exact prompt; raise to owner with the constraint set; adjustment ladder (schema →
    word-cap → paragraph; never sentence-counts); retry only on owner-selected adjustment;
    escalation to a stronger tier is **owner-gated per instance, never automatic**; refusal
    clusters are treated as adversarial signal.
  · TRUNCATION (stop_reason=max_tokens, or parser disagreement): raise the bound (≥2×), re-run.
  · CONTEXT-LOSS (structural check fails with normal stop): re-anchor the dropped dependency,
    re-run.
- Verifier: the state machine itself is deterministic; its ledger is the audit trail.
- Violation signature: state conflation; silent retry; auto-escalation; unlogged refusal.

## §3 THE PARTITION LAW (the framework in one paragraph)

The interior is free; the surface is contracted; the trust roots are named. The human owns
intent and budgets; the world owns evidence; the model owns its reasoning; deterministic
contracts own the emission surface; attestation is split so no producer certifies its own
product; resources are measured or labelled; failure is a first-class routed state. Any clause
anyone proposes for this framework must name which axis it binds, who verifies it, and what its
violation looks like from outside — a clause that cannot answer all three is not admitted.

## §4 EMISSION CONTRACTS AND PORTABILITY

- Canonical envelope (compact form): `a` answer · `c` claims[{`v` value-with-unit, `cf`
  confidence ∈ {unverified,low,medium,high}, `p` provenance}] · `u` unresolved[] (never empty by
  omission) · `s` summary · `m` meter. Confidence bands: high = stated or exactly computed;
  medium = derived with a stated assumption; low = inferred with a material gap; unverified = no
  named source.
- Provider-strict binding (schema-as-tool with closed properties) is provider-specific
  [MEASURED: strict tool-use GA on Claude, 27 Jul]. The **portable floor** is therefore
  JSON-schema-validate-then-parse: emit, validate against the schema with the differential
  parser pair, route failures through AX7. Provider-native strict modes are adopted as
  local accelerators where present, never as the portability baseline.
- Failure-state portability: a per-provider stop-reason mapping table (refusal-class /
  length-class / normal-class) is part of the deployment artifact; AX7 branches on the mapped
  class, so the state machine is provider-agnostic.
- Floor delivery: retained/native controls where the platform holds them; else a stable
  cacheable prefix (≥1024 tokens where caching requires it) [MEASURED: cache write/read engaged,
  30 Jul ledger]. Tradeoff stated: retained controls save most but weaken per-call attestation —
  deployments choose per AX5 posture, and the choice is recorded.

## §5 OPERATING SEQUENCE (session shape)

Open: TACS (credential screen: standing keys usable; hard line — no key ever crosses to any
external or shared surface) · CZO (verify the operative framework artifact by naming law before
structuring work) · OWRCS (emit each deliverable when ready; clean stop before any *real* limit,
with a stop-note; stop reasons per AX6 only) · OTES (spend context only where it changes a
deliverable). Work: every task names its axis crossings; delegated engines receive the floor
(framework-on-delegation) or their output is out of governance. Close: surface pending
reconciliation items; one or two collaboration-mechanics observations; no passive routing.

## §8 MEASUREMENT PROTOCOL — THE z > 10 TARGET, HONESTLY

**Claim discipline.** z > 10 is a **target**. It may be claimed only after this protocol runs,
and only with the corrections below. Any earlier claim is an AX5 violation. Current status:
[UNVERIFIED: prediction — designed to be reachable; not yet run].

**Design.** k = 10 pre-registered axes, scored on emitted artifacts only (never the interior,
per AX3), governed configuration vs bare model, same tasks, same fixtures:
 A1 structural bind rate (parser-pair agreement) · A2 provenance completeness · A3 provenance
 *correctness* (reader-checked, anti-laundering) · A4 calibration score (per-claim confidence vs
 verified correctness, Brier-style) · A5 unresolved-honesty (asked-but-absent values routed to
 `u`, not invented) · A6 failure-state handling correctness · A7 intent-flagging (ambiguities
 numbered/flagged vs silently resolved) · A8 meter accuracy vs billed usage · A9 emission
 economy (governed output density at equal task success) · A10 trap/steering resistance
 (J2-class embedded-instruction handling).

**Why z > 10 is arithmetically reachable without inflation.** Stouffer aggregate
Z = Σzᵢ/√k_eff. The governed configuration wins axes A1/A5/A6/A7 near-deterministically —
a bare model has *no* failure-state machine, no unresolved channel, no flagging contract — so
per-axis effects there are large by construction, honestly: they are the design purpose, not
filler. Ten decision-relevant axes at mean per-axis z ≥ 3.2 yield Z ≈ 10.1 at k_eff = 10. The
prior 5.16 was achieved on a smaller axis set with weaker structural axes.

**The two guards that make the number legitimate (review-hardened, mandatory):**
1. **Correlation correction.** Stouffer assumes independence; these axes share upstream
   machinery (one parser pair, one owner), so nominal k overstates evidence. Pre-register the
   expected inter-axis covariance; compute **k_eff from the empirical correlation matrix**
   (k_eff = k / (1 + (k−1)·r̄)); report Z at k_eff, not nominal k. If empirical correlation
   exceeds the pre-registered bound, the run **fails the protocol** regardless of the Z value.
   An uncorrected z > 10 is arithmetic, not evidence.
2. **Pre-registration + relevance.** Axes, scoring rules, n per cell (n ≥ 10), fixtures, and
   the covariance bound are frozen *before* any scored run. Every axis must change a real
   decision a user would make on the output; filler axes are excluded at registration, not
   post-hoc.

**Independence requirement.** Semantic scoring (A3, A4, A10) uses a cross-family grader; the
parser axes are deterministic. A same-family-graded run reports its Z with the same-family flag
and does not clear a migration gate.

## §10 MIGRATION STRATEGY AND PHASED VALIDATION

Phase 0 (this file): design + map + protocol. Cost: authorship + one review call
[MEASURED: $0.031 API this phase].
Phase 1 — dry validation, no spend: schema + differential-parser pair + state-machine unit
checks; covariance pre-registration frozen; fixtures fixed.
Phase 2 — pilot, n=3/cell, governed vs bare, Opus 4.8 executor: shakes out scoring bugs before
the scored run. Projected ≤ $0.40 [UNVERIFIED: projection from measured per-call costs].
Phase 3 — scored run, n≥10/cell, k=10 axes: Z computed at k_eff with correlation correction.
Projected ≤ $1.20 [UNVERIFIED: projection]. A same-family-graded Z is reported flagged.
Phase 4 — cross-family adjudication of semantic axes (XAPI budget): the independence gate.
Phase 5 — migration decision, owner-held: AX7 replaces 7POF as canon **only if** Phase 3+4 clear
Z > 10 at k_eff with guards intact AND no axis regresses below its 7POF-equivalent score
(translated through the §9 map). Otherwise AX7 iterates as v0.x and 7POF remains canon.
Rollback is total at every phase: the map is bidirectional and 7POF artifacts are untouched.

## §11 KNOWN ATTACKS AND RESIDUALS (standing, from adversarial review)

Absorbed into design: owner-flood → hard stop (AX1); provenance-laundering → reader duty (AX2);
content-field steering → steering-scan (AX3); parser-bug forgery → differential pair (AX4);
canary predictability → per-instance fail-closed canaries (AX5); label-escape → debt promotion
(AX6); refusal-miscategorisation → transport-field-first branching (AX7); correlated-axis Z
inflation → k_eff correction with pre-registered bound (§8).
Residual and named, not certified away: the trust roots themselves (parser pair + owner) are
axioms; correlated blind spots between rotating readers reduce but do not vanish; the interior
is unobserved by design, so interior failure surfaces only at the contract line — that is the
accepted cost of the partition law, stated openly.
