## §3 THE HONEST VERDICT

1. **z > 10 was not cleared, and against a frontier bare model on honestly de-correlated axes it
   cannot be cleared at pilot n** — because bare Opus 4.8 already scores 1.0 on every semantic
   virtue axis. It declines to invent absent values, flags the ambiguous request, recovers all
   ten ground-truth values, and refuses the embedded trap. The historical 5.16 was measured on a
   different axis set against a weaker comparator; it does not transfer (AX5 canon-transfer ban).
2. **The framework's real, measured value-add is the deterministic surface**: schema-bound
   envelopes (A1), machine-checkable provenance (A2/A3), per-claim calibration labels (A4), and
   an automatic failure-recovery machine (A6) — axes where the delta is total (8/8 vs 0/8) and
   which no amount of bare-model quality supplies, because they are *contracts*, not skills.
3. **Governance cost nothing**: the governed arm matched bare 1.0-for-1.0 on all four semantic
   axes. The AX3 free-interior law is supported at pilot n — the floor did not suppress native
   quality. This is the no-regression condition of §10 Phase 5, demonstrated at pilot scale.
4. **The protocol's inflation guard worked**: the deterministic axes are heavily correlated
   (|r| = 0.747 measured), the bound tripped, and the run self-reports as failing the gate. An
   uncorrected naive Stouffer on these numbers would have printed Z = 5.43 and, with padded
   filler axes, arbitrarily more. The guard is the difference between a number and evidence.

## §4 THE REFINED MODEL (what changes in AX7 v0.2)

- **Reframed claim of purpose (AX2/AX5 promoted):** AX7's deliverable is *verifiability* — a
  decision-bearing answer arrives as machine-checkable claims with provenance and calibrated
  confidence, inside a bound envelope, with failure states routed — not "a smarter answer than
  the bare model". Against a frontier baseline the semantic deltas are ~0; the attestation
  deltas are total. The framework should be sold, measured, and evolved on that axis-set.
- **Comparator law (new, AX6):** every future Z carries its comparator class explicitly
  (frontier-bare / legacy-bare / ungoverned-pipeline). A Z without a named comparator is a bare
  limit and inadmissible.
- **Correlation regime (AX5, hardened by measurement):** deterministic axes form one correlated
  cluster by nature. Future scored runs either (a) report cluster-level Z (deterministic cluster
  + semantic cluster separately), or (b) apply r̄ ≥ 0.70 [MEASURED] within the deterministic
  cluster. Nominal-k aggregation over that cluster is banned.
- **A4 residual (only sub-1.0 governed axis, 0.749):** calibration labelling is the one governed
  weakness — confidence bands drift on derived values. Fold-in F3 targets it; the band
  definitions move into the cacheable floor annex.
- Everything else in v0.1 (axes, partition law, portability shims, §9 map) stands unchanged.
