## z-score caveat (honest)
A true z is **inadmissible here**: the CONTROL baseline has zero variance (uniform 0.000), so z→∞ (division by ~0) — a degenerate statistic, not reported. gov_mean is the valid metric. A proper governance z needs a variance-bearing baseline (e.g. a weak-prompt control) or a binomial/permutation test; and n=15/edition is below a robust inferential floor — treat gov_mean as indicative ranking, raw counts as primary (R2).
