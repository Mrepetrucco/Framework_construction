   input_call_table and zblock schema.

## C. Zblock/compact schema note (both components)
The compact envelope (a/c/u/s·m) is the wire format for governed ops; full Zblock v2.1 remains
the canonical rich schema. Mapping is 1:1 on shared fields (answer→a, claims→c with
v/cf/p, unresolved→u, summary→s, meter→m); components accept either and emit compact by
default under the low-cost standard.
